2. CODE READ (candidate_1.py; candidate_2.py differs only in the docstring and PARAMS)
  Look-ahead: none found. All indicators are built in Feat from the panel passed to make(); every lookup is at row t;
    nothing is cached at module level; no centred window, no whole-sample statistic, nothing fitted. Position.hi/lo are
    updated by the harness only up to bar t. Independent re-check of all 485 / 527 entries against the stated rules
    (12 h return, 120 h high, BTC gate, EMA sign and a cross in the previous 4 bars): 0 violations.
  Fill model: market orders only, filled at the next open with spread, slippage and the taker / short fee. No limit, no
    marketable-limit trick, no stop or take level attached to any order or position.
  Accounting: gross exposure never exceeds equity (max 1.00-1.01 marked to market, mean 0.36 balanced / 0.62 aggressive);
    cash never negative; at most 8 positions (balanced) / 7 (aggressive). Each leg's cap is 24.75% of equity, so the two
    legs together could ask for 198% of equity; the shared-cash check and the harness cap stop that. Equity is not counted
    twice. Realised loss per trade against equity at entry: balanced breakout worst -1.76% (target 1%), EMA worst -1.11%
    (target 0.5%); aggressive -3.48% (target 2%) and -2.23% (target 1%). Stops judged on the close overshoot the stated
    risk by up to about 2x.
  Minor: "within 3 bars of the cross" is the cross bar plus the next 3 (age <= 3). The file carries unused code paths
    (hedge, intrabar stop, EMA regime switch); none is active with the frozen PARAMS.
