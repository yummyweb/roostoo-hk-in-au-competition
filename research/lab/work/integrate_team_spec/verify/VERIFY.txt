2. CODE READING (ts.py, candidate_1.py, candidate_2.py)
  No look-ahead found. All indicators come from ind.* on the panel passed to make; on_bar reads row t only; the K-Means
  model for rows [end, end+336) is fitted and standardised on rows < end; refit schedule is anchored to the panel, not
  to the start bar. The module-level _MEMO cache is keyed on panel identity, so an altered panel is always refitted.
  No fill-model abuse: B sends market orders only. Sizing uses equity once per order; the two budgets can add up to
  149% of equity but buys are capped by free cash (63 rejected or shrunk orders in B, 9 in A), so there is no leverage.
  Exits are listed before entries, so cash freed by a sale funds the same hour's buys (a live bot must send in that order).
  Small defects, none material:
   - Both legs write to one cooldown dict, so a mean-reversion exit also blocks the EMA leg on that coin for 12 bars
     (and an EMA cross-exit blocks the MR leg for 6). With separate dicts B is +83.7% (was +84.8%).
   - A mean-reversion holding blocks an EMA entry in that coin (one position per coin) and the 3-bar window then
     expires. Stated in the description; effect not separable from the MR leg's contribution.
   - Start-bar dependence is small: starting at 744/756/768/792/840/912 gives +82% to +85%.
