2. CODE READING (mix.py, candidate_1.py, candidate_2.py)
  No look-ahead found. Every feature is built inside Mix.__init__ from the panel passed in (ATR, EMA48/200, BTC EMA480,
  12 h return, 120 h rolling max that includes bar t). on_bar(t) reads rows <= t and Position.hi/lo, which the harness
  updates through bar t only. Nothing is cached at module level. No whole-sample statistic.
  No abuse of the fill model: all orders are market orders filled at the next open; there are no limits, stops or takes.
  Sizing: each buy = equity x 0.67 x 0.99 / 4; EMA size = min(slot, 0.0067 x equity x close / (6 x ATR)). Matches the text.
  One real inaccuracy in the description, not a bug that inflates results:
   - The two "separate capital sleeves" add up to 134% of equity (both read the whole account's equity). The harness
     forbids leverage, so the legs share cash. Measured in C1: gross exposure mean 44%, p90 88%, max 100.5% of equity;
     free cash was below half a slot (entry refused) in 8.2% of hours; 14 of 134 breakout buys and 3 of 348 EMA entries
     were cut to about 75% of size; the breakout leg made 133 trades in the mix versus 148 alone. Shorts lock their
     collateral as cash. The live bot must reproduce this cash rule (entry only if half its size is free; exits first).
  Small things: the "12 h lockout" blocks 11 further decision bars; the EMA "600 bars of history" test is on the bar
  index, not per coin (same thing on this panel).

5. IS THE MIX BETTER THAN ITS BEST LEG?
  Whole period: yes on the ratios. C1 2.79 / 6.73 / 16.4 versus breakout alone 2.13 / 5.13 / 9.00 (at 67%: 2.09 / 4.97 / 7.16)
  and EMA alone 2.36 / 4.71 / 8.72 (at 67%: 2.37 / 4.73 / 7.92). 94% / 77% / 90% of the 31 C1 neighbours beat C2 on
  Sharpe / Sortino / Calmar. It is ordinary diversification: daily-return correlation of the two sleeves +0.26, and simply
  adding the two standalone 67% sleeves gives Sharpe 2.67, Sortino 6.34.
  Corrections to the claim:
   a. "Beats each alone on drawdown" is wrong for the EMA leg: EMA alone has 12.9% (8.7% at 67%), the mix 16.5%.
   b. The advantage comes entirely from the first two thirds. In the last (most recent) third the mix made +2% with
      Sharpe 0.31 / Sortino 0.54 while the breakout alone made +12% with 1.18 / 2.78; the EMA leg lost 8% there. No C1
      neighbour beat C2 in that third.
   c. The fresh 11-day MEAN is the same (+4.53% vs +4.50%). What the mix buys is shape: median +2.6% vs 0, wins 71% vs 45%,
      worst -7.8% vs -13.2%, fills on 79% vs 40% of days.
  What the short side is: EMA shorts alone have beta -0.17 to the equal-weight market, lose 0.41% a day on up days and
  earn 0.55% on down days; $29k of their $38k came in two falling months (Nov 2025, Feb 2026). The mean coin fell in 7 of
  the 10 months. EMA longs lost about $21k in Jan-Mar 2026. The mix with EMA shorts only was +141%, 2.64 / 6.56 / 14.0 with
  thirds +54 / +38 / +13: on design the long half of the EMA leg adds return in the middle third and costs it in the last.
  No sustained rising market exists in this panel, so the short leg's cost in a rally is untested here.
